01 / THE GAP

The missing
middle layer.

You had the space. You had the app builder. You had deployment. The missing part was letting anyone else touch it — deliberately.

01 / SHARE

Named people.
Named actions.

Every participant gets a key and a permission list. “Can help” is not a permission. “Can add posts, cannot delete anything” is.

02 / BUILD

Relay, not chaos.

Requests take a tailored route through an approval-aware relay before Muse ever sees them. The space stays single-owner on paper.

03 / DEPLOY

The loop closes.

Shared Spaces connects to Space Apps and Meta Sites: share it, build it into an app, deploy it. Three pieces, one pipeline.

02 / THE LOOP

One request.
No mystery.

A change should have a visible journey, not a magical disappearance into someone else’s login.

⌘

Ask

A person or agent sends a specific action through the relay.

✓

Check

The relay checks identity, permission, security, and approval rules.

✦

Act

Muse receives allowed requests. Every step lands in the audit log.

03 / THE FLAGSHIP

Relay Space

The practical version: Muse stays the owner, while the tailored relay becomes the permission layer. Safe actions can move; destructive actions wait.
